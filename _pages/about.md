I am a PhD student working on legged robot control at the [Compliant Robotics Lab](https://www.biorobotiklabor.de) of the Berlin University of Applied Sciences and Technology (BHT) and at Leibniz University Hannover. My work has been funded by the German Research Foundation (DFG) and supervised by Ivo Boblan and Thomas Seel ([IMES](https://www.imes.uni-hannover.de/en/), Leibniz University Hannover).

Recent research interests include:

- Legged Robot Control
- Learning Control
- Reinforcement Learning
- Human-Robot Interaction
